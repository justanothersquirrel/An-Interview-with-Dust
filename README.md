# An Interview with Dust

performance research tool, part of on going research on the notion of dust.
“An Interview with Dust” is a code project built to elaborate an act of investigation with dust as part of Amorphous Research. Performing the investigation with the endoscope and audio triggering has established a condition of communication and experimentation with found objects (dirt and thrown-away items), dust from the vacuum machine, and the Temple of the Sun, a rock from a Roman temple in Lebanon.
The experiment illuminates an interview with dust, giving this matter the stage and opportunity to explain and elaborate its existence. This audio and visual participation has made available close, awakened, and random engagement with abandoned, fallen, unnoticed objects… This is an unfinished experiment.
